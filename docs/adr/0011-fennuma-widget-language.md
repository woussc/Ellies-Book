# ADR 0011: Fennuma and the compact widget language

Status: Accepted on 20 November 2026, following SHUAI's brand choice and Jaz's visual-language v1 handoff.

Fennuma is the public name. Ellie's Book remains the internal repository and storage/schema identifier. Existing backups and device keys do not change.

Supersede the earlier typography, palette and board geometry decisions only with Manrope, porcelain/ink/sage/deep-clay tokens, role-shaped compact widgets, exact readable money, local collision-free movement and contextual relationship links. Preserve the warm, light direction and bans on gradients, glass, glows, ambient motion and resting shadows. Reference sizes: Standard 160×104, Compact 128×88, Signal 104×88, Circle 104×104, Pill 176×64, Wide 208×104; content and text scaling can require larger representations.

Board, Plan, Log, Books and Profile remain the approved navigation. The three-tab design illustration does not replace it. Scope and USD stay explicit. Aggregate contribution links are dashed and labeled; real category/entry and Markdown links are solid. Layout never changes financial relationships, values, lifecycle or audit records.

The compact Overview is the initial Board at every viewport, so financial summaries lead the first fold. All widgets and individual lifecycle clusters remain explicit choices with independent saved cameras. Semantic hit bounds stay at least 44×44 screen pixels without enlarging a role's visual shape or changing its position.

Verification includes responsive live interaction, exact-money containment, persistence/invariance, keyboard and reduced motion. Design artifacts are not test evidence. Physical iOS/Safari checks remain a separate gate when unavailable on this machine.
